OPENMAIL / QUARTERLY ROADMAP WORKSHOP
Internal working notes

CUSTOMER OUTCOMES

1. Make the first connected account feel immediately useful.
   - Reduce time from signup to first meaningful action.
   - Preserve familiar folder names and existing labels.
   - Explain account colors without adding another onboarding screen.

2. Keep account separation obvious across every interaction.
   - Sender lines retain account color in conversation view.
   - Search results stay scoped to the current user.
   - Attachments, drafts, and scheduled messages never cross accounts.

3. Give people fewer reasons to revisit the same message.
   - Sensible snooze presets for later today and next week.
   - Predictable archive and undo behavior.
   - Read states that stay consistent between list and detail views.

PLATFORM PRIORITIES

- Provider token encryption and independent failure recovery.
- Incremental synchronization with bounded retries and jitter.
- Accessible keyboard navigation and reliable focus restoration.
- Account-scoped attachment preview URLs and cache keys.

OPEN QUESTIONS

- Which search operators should be visible before the external beta?
- Should scheduled sending default to the sender's local timezone?
- Do custom labels need colors in the initial release?
